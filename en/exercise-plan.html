Exercises

1
Chair Sit-to-Stand
Leg strength · main exercise
3 sets8–10 reps2 min rest
How
  • Sit on the edge of a chair, feet hip-width apart.
  • Arms crossed over your chest — do not push off with them.
  • Stand up over 3 seconds, sit down with control over 3 seconds.
Caution
  • Do not let your knees cave inwards.
  • Do not drop into the chair — control the descent.
Progression
  • Weeks 1–2: using your arms for support · Weeks 3–4: no arms · Week 5+: holding a weight
2
Knee-to-Wall
Ankle mobility · EVERY DAY
Every day3 sets10 repsHold 5 s
How
  • Toes 8–10 cm from the wall, foot pointing straight at the wall.
  • Push your knee towards the wall without lifting your heel.
  • Hold for 5 seconds, then return.
Caution
  • If your heel lifts, shorten the distance — otherwise you are cheating.
  • Do 1 extra set on the left side (asymmetry).
Target
  • Currently 7 cm → 9–10 cm in 12 weeks
3
Heel Raises
Calf strength · single leg
3 sets12–15 repssingle leg
How
  • Stand on one leg, touching the wall lightly for balance.
  • Raise your heel slowly and hold for 1 s at the top.
  • Lower over 3 seconds — the lowering phase is what really counts.
Caution
  • Do not lean on the wall; touch it only with your fingertips.
  • Do the left foot first, then match the number of reps on the right to the left.
Progression
  • Weeks 1–3: both feet · Week 4+: single leg · Week 8+: on the edge of a step
4
Goblet Squat
Leg strength · loaded
3 sets10 reps4–8 kg
How
  • Hold the weight (dumbbell/kettlebell) in front of your chest with both hands.
  • Sit back by pushing your hips backwards, chest up.
  • Lower until your thighs are parallel to the floor, then push through your heels to stand up.
Caution
  • Keep the natural curve in your lower back — do not let your back round.
  • If there is pain, reduce the depth, not the load.
Progression
  • 5% load increase every 2 weeks (4 → 6 → 8 kg)
5
Calf Stretch (Wall)
Flexibility · EVERY DAY
Every day2 sets30 sboth sides
How
  • Place your hands on the wall and step the leg to be stretched back.
  • Back heel on the floor, knee straight — this stretches the gastrocnemius.
  • Then bend the back knee slightly — this time the soleus is stretched. Do both.
Caution
  • No forced or bouncing stretches — hold still.
  • You should feel tension, not pain.
Note
  • Do this after exercise (not before).
6
Banded Hip Abduction
Side hip muscles · balance support
3 sets12 repslight band
How
  • Band just above your ankles; stand up straight.
  • Take your leg out to the side, hold for 2 seconds, then bring it back with control.
  • Keep your trunk still — do not lean sideways.
Caution
  • The leg should go straight out to the side, not forwards.
  • 1 extra set on the left leg.
Why
  • Hip abductors were below norm at assessment (79%/90%).
7
Single-Leg Balance
Balance · maintenance
3 sets30 seach leg
How
  • Stand on one leg, with the other knee slightly raised.
  • Keep your gaze on a fixed point. Your arms help with balance.
Progression
  • Weeks 1–4: eyes open, firm surface
  • Weeks 5–8: eyes open, on a cushion
  • Week 9+: turning your head or catching a ball
Note
  • Balance is already good (16 s) — this is done to maintain it.
8
Step-Ups
Functional strength
3 sets8 reps20 cm step
How
  • Place one foot on the step and step up pushing through the heel.
  • Do not push off with the back foot — the front leg does the work.
  • Step down with control; complete all 8 reps on the same leg.
Caution
  • Hold the handrail for support if needed.
  • Do not let your knee travel too far past your toes.
Progression
  • 20 cm → 30 cm step → holding a weight
9
Fast Sit-to-Stand
POWER · from week 5
Target exercise3 sets6–8 reps3 min rest
How
  • Stand up from the chair as fast as you can (explosively).
  • When sitting down, lower yourself slowly and with control (3 s).
  • If your speed drops, end the set — do not keep going when fatigued.
Why this exercise
  • Sit-to-stand power is below the threshold at 1.8 W/kg. Power is lost faster than strength, and it is power that really prevents falls. The most critical part of the programme.
Start
  • Not done in weeks 1–4 (foundation strength first). Added in week 5.

Load progression rule: If you complete the target reps for an exercise without struggling in 2 sessions in a row, increase the load by 5%. Sign of struggling: clear slowing of the last 2 reps. If there is pain, do not increase the load — first check your technique and range of motion.
